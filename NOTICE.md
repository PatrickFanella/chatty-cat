Chatty Cat includes the curated resource lists, emoji search keywords, display
palettes, presets and font list first published in HasanAbi Chat Thing
(https://github.com/mxmilkiib/hasanabi.neocities.org) under AGPL-3.0. Those items
keep their original attribution and licenses.

The source is maintained at:
https://github.com/subculture-collective/chatty-cat

When operating a modified version for remote users, provide access to its
corresponding source as required by the license. See LICENSE for its terms.

Unicode Emoji 17.0 names, sequences and categories are derived from Unicode, Inc.'s
emoji-test.txt under Unicode License v3. The pinned source, license and SHA256
are in scripts/data/unicode-17 and docs/emoji-sources.json.

Local Twemoji 17.0.3 SVG graphics are by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors,
maintained at https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji. They are copied unchanged under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The graphics license is included
at web/public/emoji/twemoji-17.0.3/LICENSE-GRAPHICS.txt and accessible in the picker.
The pinned archive and per-asset SHA256 values are in docs/emoji-sources.json.
